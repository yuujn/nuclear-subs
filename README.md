# Nuclear Subs (Capstone Project, Week 8)
This is a Point of Sale application produced by and for a fictitious restaurant known
by the name "Nuclear Subs", which sells submarine sandwiches.

Do not actually use it!

## Nuclear Subs
Welcome to Nuclear Subs!

This is our Point of Sale application, which presents our menu in a friendly
terminal based UI, and allows customers to enter and modify their orders to
the maximum extent our cooks are willing to deal with.

That means...

...Drink selection, flavor and size.

...Chips, what type they are.

...Sandwiches, bread, meat, cheese, lettuce, almost any other topping under the sun, sauces,
and whether they want a bit more than usual of any of those.

It also supports ordering sandwiches from a selection of recipes we see a lot,
which we call our "Signature" sandwiches, and customization of those after their selection.
